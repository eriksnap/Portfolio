Developer Story

I am a game development student with a passion for building interactive systems that challenge and engage players. My interest in games goes beyond just playing them. I want to understand how they work, why they feel the way they do, and how to make them better.

I work under the studio name Hollow Echo Studios and have built projects ranging from experimental control-based puzzle games to 2D platformers. I enjoy the full process, from initial design and programming through to testing and polish.

My goal is to ship games that feel intentional and well-crafted, and to grow into a professional role in the games industry.
